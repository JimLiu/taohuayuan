## Meshes from the reference scene (Sakura River Valley, valley.mengto.here.now)

`assets-src/web/ref/*.msh` are the reference page's own compressed meshes (its `assets/*.msh-*.gz`, un-gzipped, format unchanged). They ship gzipped again as `src/assets/ref/*.mshz` (same bytes once unpacked).
The reference's own credits (an HTML comment in its index.html) name Poly Haven (CC0) for its scanned rocks, ferns and roots, so the rock and fern meshes below are CC0 in origin.
No credit or licence was found in the page's files for the three figures (boatman, woman, monk): they are used here **for personal study only**. Replace them before any public or commercial release.

| file | what it is | used as |
|---|---|---|
| boatman.msh | skinned fisherman in hat and straw cape | 渔人 (all chapters); repainted procedurally in `src/actors/skinned.js` |
| monk.msh | sculpted old monk sweeping with a besom | 黄发 elder sweeping the lane; repainted, grey hair and a topknot added, in `src/actors/elder.js` |
| rock_moss_set_02_lo.msh | the reference's low-poly bake of Poly Haven Rock Moss Set 02 (CC0) | mossy boulders along the creek (`src/world/banks.js`) |
| fern_02.msh | the reference's bake of Poly Haven Fern 02 (CC0) | ferns along the creek (`src/world/banks.js`) |

The reference's textures for these were placeholders, so the rock and fern use the Poly Haven CC0 originals above and the two figures are painted by region in code.

Not used and kept out of the build (`assets-src/ref-unused/`): woman.msh (her parasol and red paper read Japanese), rock_moss_set_02.msh (high-poly), rock_moss_set_01.msh, rock_face_01(_lo).msh, root_cluster_01.msh.
